# Thundergif 1.1.0

## New

- Switching between GIPHY and KLIPY keeps your search and runs it on the other Provider. If you are looking at Favourites, you stay there.
- GIPHY results now show GIPHY's own "Powered by GIPHY" badge, and each GIF credits its creator in its hover text where GIPHY names one.
- A "Buy me a beer" link at the bottom of the settings page, for anyone who wants to say thanks. It never pops up.

## Good to know

- Favourites are Thundergif's own. GIFs saved in your GIPHY or KLIPY account cannot be imported, because neither offers apps a way to read them.
- Runs on Thunderbird 140 to 158. Tested on Thunderbird 158 on macOS.

## Install

Download `thundergif-1.1.0.xpi` below, then in Thunderbird open Tools > Add-ons and Themes > gear icon > Install Add-on From File.
